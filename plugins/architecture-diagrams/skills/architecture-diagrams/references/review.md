# Reviewing a diagram

Two reviewers that did not draw the diagram check it separately, after you have rendered it
and fixed what you saw yourself. Start each as a fresh subagent with only the brief below,
the render, and what the brief names. Without subagents, run both checklists yourself, one
after the other, and say so.

Each reviewer returns a list of findings, each with what is wrong, where (the element or
area), and the fix. "Nothing found" is a valid answer. Fix every finding you agree with,
say why for any you reject, and render again.

## Style reviewer

Give it: the render as an image, the theme values, the craft rules from SKILL.md, and the
approved examples as images if the project has them.

Brief: "Check this diagram against the theme and craft rules. Look for:

- shapes, strokes, fonts or colours that differ from the theme, or from the examples;
- a layout that runs the wrong way (sequential diagrams run top to bottom) or is wider than
  one screen;
- an icon that is missing, too small, off-centre over its label, or not the best fit for
  its concept, and the same concept drawn with two icons;
- connectors with gaps, floating labels, labels that are not verbs, or crossings that a
  different placement would avoid;
- an element with more than one link marker, or a link on a label;
- text under 16 px, overlaps, leftovers, and a missing or incomplete legend."

## Content reviewer

Give it: the render, what the diagram is meant to show, and the sources or records it rests
on (or their text).

Brief: "Check that this diagram says what its sources say. Look for:

- an element, relation or label the sources do not support, or that contradicts them;
- something the sources say that the diagram should show and does not;
- the same thing shown twice, or more than one idea in one diagram;
- names that differ from the terms the sources use;
- a form that does not suit what it shows (see the form table)."
